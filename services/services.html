IRS Representation

Should you need representation before the IRS, JustRightTax is here to help. Our team will represent you with integrity and professionalism, ensuring that your interests are effectively communicated and your tax matters are handled properly.

ITIN Preparation

For individuals without a Social Security Number, the IRS provides an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number), used solely for tax purposes. JustRightTax can assist you in obtaining your ITIN and filing your taxes using it. Additionally, we offer COA (Certificate of Authorization) services, eliminating the need for an in-person visit to the IRS.

1031 Exchange

If you own property in India or are considering buying or selling property, JustRightTax can assist you with the 1031 Exchange process. We simplify this tax-deferred exchange, ensuring you can defer paying capital gains taxes on the property sale while reinvesting in similar property. Let us guide you through this often complex process with ease.
